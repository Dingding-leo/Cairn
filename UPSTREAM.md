# Upstream Provenance

## ai-berkshire

Cairn was originally informed by the investment-research workflow project:

- Repository: `https://github.com/xbtlin/ai-berkshire`
- Audit baseline commit: `f98eff38d01a17a39d95940709afce61e75c8ac7`
- License: MIT
- Required attribution: `LICENSES/ai-berkshire-MIT.txt`

## Concepts adapted

Cairn retains and adapts useful upstream ideas including:

- explicit investment checklists;
- thesis assumptions and invalidation conditions;
- thesis drift / evidence-change discipline;
- source-gap declarations;
- deterministic calculation rather than LLM mental arithmetic;
- generated compatibility artifacts as a concept.

## Concepts intentionally rewritten

The crypto derivative does not treat upstream prompt behavior as an implementation of portfolio management or trading. Cairn rewrites or separates:

- authoritative numeric validation;
- persistent state;
- skills versus agents versus workflows;
- crypto-specific supply and holder-value capture;
- category-specific valuation;
- OKX market data and market-structure analysis;
- paper accounting;
- model-quality experimentation;
- future deterministic risk and execution boundaries.

## Concepts intentionally not carried forward

- equity-only PE/ROE filters as universal token rules;
- famous-investor personas as evidence or authority;
- hard-coded stock concentration guidance;
- automatic mapping of qualitative confidence to position size;
- Markdown as the only system of record;
- multi-agent debate as an assumed source of accuracy.

MIT permission allows reuse and modification provided the copyright and permission notice are retained in substantial copies. Cairn keeps that notice explicitly in `LICENSES/`.
