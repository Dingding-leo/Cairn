# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Cairn is a technical research project. Contributors are expected to communicate professionally, critique evidence and designs rather than people, disclose uncertainty, and make room for disagreement that can be resolved through tests or sources.

Examples of constructive behavior include:

- giving specific, reproducible technical feedback;
- distinguishing facts, inferences, and proposals;
- acknowledging errors and updating conclusions when evidence changes;
- respecting privacy, security boundaries, and responsible disclosure;
- avoiding performance claims that the evidence does not support.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate security abuse, credential exposure, market manipulation, or knowingly presenting fabricated research or test results as genuine.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that violate this standard. Serious security or privacy issues should be reported privately as described in `SECURITY.md` rather than litigated in public threads.

## Attribution

This is a concise project-specific conduct policy. Projects preferring the full Contributor Covenant may replace it through a documented governance change.
